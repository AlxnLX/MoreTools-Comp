# 🛠️ More Tools

> **⚠️ Important Requirements:**
> 
> *   **v1.1x:** Requires **JEI** (Just Enough Items) and **Curios API** to function correctly.
> *   **v1.0:** These dependencies are completely optional.

***

## **Expand your Minecraft experience with brand-new tools, minerals, and unique items!**

**More Tools** introduces a wide variety of materials, weapons, and blocks designed to enhance your survival worlds while keeping the classic vanilla charm intact.

***

## 🔹 **Main Features**

*   ⛏️ **Rare Ores:** Discover new, valuable minerals hidden deep underground.
*   ⚔️ **Custom Gear:** Equip exclusive tools and weapons equipped with special abilities.
*   🧱 **Decorations:** Build and customize your base with brand-new decorative and functional blocks.
*   🤝 **Mod Compatible:** Works seamlessly alongside other standard survival mods and custom crafting systems.
